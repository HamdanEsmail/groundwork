How a dossier is drawn

To begin, type the address the way you would say it. A trailing full stop, a ZIP code or lower case all land on the same parcel, and a condominium building answers with its units. An address the city does not hold is reported as no match, never guessed.

Within a minute, TinyFish Fetch reads four pages of the city atlas: assessment and sale, zoning, licences and inspections, deeds. A page that is still loading is read again. One that will not load becomes a marked gap with the link to check by hand.

On request, TinyFish Agent opens the five offices that have no simple page: assessment history, the tax balance, the flood map, catchment schools and the full permit history. Each read is capped, and its price is shown before it starts.

From then on, TinyFish Monitor checks the licences page and the assessment page of a watched address twice a day. A new permit, a new violation, a sale or a changed assessment is recorded with what the page said before and after.
